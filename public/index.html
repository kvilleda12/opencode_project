02 Read the floor

Every possession
has a language.

01 / VISION

See the play
before it opens.

Look up before you receive the ball. Notice who is moving into space and pass where your teammate is going.

Read.THEN REACT

02 / DECISION

Catch. Read.
Move.

On the catch, check the rim, your defender, and an open teammate. Shoot, pass, or drive with purpose.

03 / RHYTHM

Find the beat.

Practice changing pace with a controlled dribble. Slow down to read your defender, then accelerate into open space.
